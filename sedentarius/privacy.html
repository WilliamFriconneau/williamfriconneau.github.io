Sedentarius — Privacy policy

Sedentarius collects no personal data.

  • The app reads Health data (stand hours, steps, flights climbed, activity, exercise and workouts, wheelchair use, heart rate, time in daylight, sleep) on your iPhone and Apple Watch to show your day of standing. The watch saves your 1-minute breaks to Health as workouts, with their energy and heart rate.
  • This data never leaves your devices. The iPhone and the watch exchange it directly, through Apple's WatchConnectivity.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party services.
  • Purchases go through Apple. The developer receives neither your card nor your identity.
  • Links to sources open in your browser.
  • Your data stays on your devices, in Health and in the app's own storage, for as long as the app is installed.
  • Deleting the app deletes its own data. The breaks it saved stay in Health, where you can delete them.
  • You can withdraw access at any time: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Sedentarius. Reminders: Settings › Notifications › Sedentarius.
  • Not a medical device: Sedentarius doesn't diagnose or treat anything. If you have a health condition, ask your doctor before changing your activity.

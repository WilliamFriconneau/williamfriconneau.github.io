Homo Sedentarius — Privacy policy

Homo Sedentarius collects no personal data.

  • The app reads Health data (stand hours, activity, exercise, time in daylight, sleep) on your iPhone and Apple Watch to show your day of standing. The watch saves your 1-minute breaks to Health as workouts, with their energy and heart rate.
  • This data never leaves your devices. The iPhone and the watch exchange it directly, through Apple's WatchConnectivity.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party services.
  • Purchases and subscriptions go through Apple. The developer receives neither your card nor your identity.
  • Links to sources open in your browser.

Questions: through the support link on the App Store page.

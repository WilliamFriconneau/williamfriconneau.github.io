Sedentarius — Support

Sedentarius shows your day of standing as a little figure, on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and reminds you to get up once an hour.

Questions

The figure doesn't update. Open the app once on the watch, and check Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Sedentarius: every item should be on.

I get two stand reminders. Turn off Apple's own stand reminders in the Watch app › Activity, or Sedentarius's reminders in its Settings.

A break started on its own. That's "Auto-start break": turn it off in the app's Settings.

I bought the full version on another device. Settings › Support development › Restore purchase.

Delete my data. Delete the app. The breaks it saved stay in the Health app, where you can delete them.

Not a medical device. If you have a health condition, ask your doctor before changing your activity.

Assistance

Une question ou un problème : ouvrez un ticket (public, compte GitHub nécessaire). Pour supprimer vos données, supprimez l'app ; les pauses enregistrées restent dans l'app Santé, où vous pouvez les supprimer.
